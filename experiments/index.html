Operating frame

These are not predictions. They are small, testable engineering bets. Each experiment should produce an artifact: a repo, a spec, a working demo, a measurement log, or a technical note.

Instrument first. Prefer traces, diffs, tests, benchmarks, and reproducible logs over intuition.
Keep loops reversible. Agentic workflows need fork, rollback, replay, and promotion semantics.
Promote only durable work. Raw ideas enter a backlog; only pressure-tested ideas graduate into specs or repos.
