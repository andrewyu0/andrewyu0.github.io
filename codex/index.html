NYC Builder Sessions

Hands-on Codex sessions for builders: bring a repo, pick a task, use Codex, inspect the diff, run tests, and leave with a PR.

Builder-session format

  • Bring a real repo. No toy-only demos.
  • Pick one bounded task. Bugfix, test, refactor, docs, or CLI improvement.
  • Use Codex in the loop. Planning, implementation, debugging, and review.
  • Inspect the diff. Humans own architecture, correctness, and final judgment.
  • Run tests and ship. The output should be reviewable, not just impressive.

Why this matters

The next phase of AI-assisted engineering will be won by builders who can combine AI velocity with software-engineering discipline: small branches, crisp specs, reproducible demos, tests, diffs, reviews, and operational judgment.
